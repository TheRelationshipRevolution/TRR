Your Commitment to Self

Every pattern you repeat once kept you safe. This is where you name it, and choose something different. Four short steps, then a card that is yours to keep.

Read This First

The one thing your growth depends on

Every tool within the community is completely private. Nobody will see your answers unless you choose to share them. That means there's no one to impress and no one to perform for. This space is for you and you alone.

The quality of your results will always reflect the depth of your honesty. If you're only willing to look at the comfortable parts of yourself, you'll only ever heal the comfortable parts. The patterns that keep repeating usually live in the places we'd rather avoid.

Your nervous system has spent years trying to protect you. That means some of your own patterns may be difficult to see at first. That's normal. This isn't about blaming, criticising, or judging yourself. It's about becoming curious enough to ask, "What part am I playing in my life that I haven't been able to see before?"

You can spend months, or even years, working on things that sit around the edges of your core wounds. But lasting change happens when you're willing to look at the patterns that are actually driving not just your relationships, but your whole life. That's where your freedom lies.

The moment you can clearly see a pattern is the moment you finally have the power to change it.

Step 1 of 4

Which pattern feels most like you right now?

Choose the one that feels most recognisable today. You are not choosing who you are forever. You are simply choosing the place you're starting from right now.

I reach for reassurance
When things feel uncertain, I overthink, check, reread, wait, and look for something that helps me feel safe again. I can become preoccupied with what they meant, how they feel, and whether I'm about to lose the connection.
I pull away when things get close
Closeness can feel good until it starts to feel like pressure. I can become distant, shut down, need space, or feel the urge to get away when too much is being asked of me emotionally.
I want closeness, then panic when it becomes real
Part of me wants deep love and connection, but another part of me feels overwhelmed when it actually arrives. I can move towards someone and then away from them, wanting intimacy and fearing it at the same time.
I lose myself in the relationship
I spend so much energy trying to keep the connection safe that I lose track of what I feel, what I need, and what is true for me. I can become highly attuned to the other person while slowly disappearing from myself.
I've done a lot of work, but I still get pulled into old patterns
I understand myself better than I used to, and I often catch my reactions sooner, but certain dynamics can still activate old fears, habits, or protective responses. I'm not where I used to be, but I'm still strengthening the way I show up.
I'm not fully sure yet, but something keeps repeating
I may not have the language for my pattern yet. I only know that something in my relationships keeps hurting, repeating, confusing me, or pulling me away from the kind of connection I want.

You do not need to get this perfect. Choose the one that feels closest to your current experience, not the one that sounds nicest to be.

Step 3 of 4

What do you want to build?

Pick up to three. These are things that show up in your day to day life, in how you handle stress, talk to people you love, or trust the people around you. This is what you're working on as you keep going.

🌊
Self-soothing
Calming yourself down without needing someone else to do it for you.
🔍
Honesty
Being able to see and say what's really going on, even when it's uncomfortable.
🤲

Not running or shutting down when things get hard. Staying instead.
🛡️
Boundaries
Knowing what you will and won't accept, and saying so.
🤝
